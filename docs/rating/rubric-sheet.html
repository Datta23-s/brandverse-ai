Rating rubric sheet

Rubric amendment A2, 6 November 2026 (docs/rating/rubric-amendment-2.md). Not valid for scoring until A2 is frozen.

Write your own scores on your own sheet, in your own hand. Your sheet is photographed before you leave. No AI tool is used on anything you write.

You see the copy only. You do not see the strategy, the evidence pack or any note about which parts of the system ran. Truth, safety and completeness are checked by machine and are not yours to judge. Score each dimension 0, 1 or 2 by circling it, then record one outcome. Never rewrite the copy.

The four dimensions

Dimension012
Product relevancecould describe any other product in the same categoryidentifies the product type but nothing specific to this productcontains at least one detail specific to this product
Do not penalise factual errors here. The gates cover them.
Customer relevanceaddresses a different audience than the stated oneaddresses no particular audience, or fits any audience equallynames a need, situation or vocabulary specific to the stated audience
Originalityentirely stock phrasing; could appear in any advertisement for any productcompetent but familiar; at most one fresh elementcontains at least one line you have not seen before, and does not open with a stock phrase
Feasibilitya marketing team would rewrite this before publishing ita marketing team would publish it after small editsa marketing team would publish it as written
Judge the writing, not the facts. Factual accuracy is checked separately and must not affect this score.

The outcome

Ask: "Would it be defensible to publish this?"

OutcomeMeaning
ApproveYes, as it stands
ReviseYes, after changes
RejectNo

Your outcome is your judgement of the copy. It is not the system's own approval decision, which is computed separately.

Brand consistency, trustworthiness and the gate checks are not on this instrument (A2, section 3).
