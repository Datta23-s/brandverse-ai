Circle one score per dimension, using the anchors on the rubric sheet. Product relevance: do not penalise factual errors. Feasibility: judge the writing, not the facts. Outcome answers "Would it be defensible to publish this?": approve (yes, as it stands), revise (yes, after changes), reject (no). Feasibility rates the WRITING; the outcome is your decision as the brand's approver, all things considered. They can differ, and often will. Never rewrite the copy. Truth, safety and completeness are machine-checked and are not yours to score.
