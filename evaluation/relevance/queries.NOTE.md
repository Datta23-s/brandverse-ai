# Note on `queries.md` — added 2026-10-25, not part of the frozen record

`queries.md` is frozen by hash in `queries.lock` and was left byte-identical on purpose.

Its header line says the queries were "reviewed and edited by Kundan". That line predates the
review it describes. Kundan read all 50 queries on 2026-10-25 and accepted them **without
changes**; no query was edited. The header was left unedited to preserve the freeze: re-freezing
a frozen artifact to fix a header is the habit freezing exists to prevent.

`docs/evaluation-plan.md` §7 ("Limitation of the relevance set") is the authoritative statement of
how the queries were written and reviewed. Where the two differ, §7 governs.
