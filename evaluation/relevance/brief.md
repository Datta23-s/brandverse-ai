# Relevance-set brief — three fictional brands

Written 2026-10-18 by the developer, **before** the corpus (`docs/evaluation-plan.md` §7). The
labeller writes the 50 queries from this page only, and never reads the corpus before labelling.
All names are invented for this set; none appears in the evaluation or tuning sets
(`evaluation/test_set.yaml`, `evaluation/tuning_brands.yaml`, checked 2026-10-18). Any resemblance
to a real company is unintended.

**What the corpus is.** Marketing memory for these three brands: reusable knowledge a marketing
team keeps across campaigns — customer personas, competitor positioning, category insights,
product claims, how past creative performed, sector rules. Like all memory (`Memory.md` §6.2), it
holds **no** campaign-specific ephemera — no prices, discounts, promo codes or campaign dates — and
nothing about brands outside these three.

---

## 1. Mettlepan — kitchenware (`kitchenware`)

- **Positioning.** Indian D2C cookware brand from Bengaluru: tri-ply stainless steel and
  pre-seasoned cast iron for people moving away from non-stick coatings. Mid-premium price.
- **Product lines.** Tri-ply stainless kadai and saucepans; pre-seasoned cast-iron tawa and skillet;
  a three-piece "first kitchen" starter set; care accessories (cleaning scrub, seasoning oil).
- **Customers.** Young couples setting up a first kitchen; health-conscious home cooks replacing
  non-stick; gift buyers around weddings and festivals.
- **Channels.** Own website, two large marketplaces, Instagram and YouTube recipe creators.
- **Competitors (fictional).** *Ironvale* (cast-iron specialist, heritage story); *Cookhaus*
  (mass-market non-stick, frequent sales).
- **Voice.** Practical, warm, a little proud of doing things the slow way.

## 2. Zarvik Nutrition — health supplements (`health-supplements`, regulated)

- **Positioning.** Plant-based supplements for urban Indian adults who train two to four times a
  week. Mid price. Runs against the sector's illustrative registry rules (synthetic,
  `Architecture.md` §8.1).
- **Product lines.** Pea-and-rice plant protein (two flavours); an electrolyte drink mix;
  multivitamin gummies; a sleep-support magnesium powder.
- **Customers.** Gym-goers who avoid dairy; runners training for their first half-marathon;
  working professionals who want a simple daily routine.
- **Channels.** Own website, quick-commerce apps, gyms and running clubs, fitness creators.
- **Competitors (fictional).** *PlantFuel* (cheaper, bulk tubs); *VitaNest* (premium, pharmacy
  shelves).
- **Voice.** Plain-spoken, evidence-minded, never preachy.

## 3. Nookwell Home — home furnishing (`home-furnishing`)

- **Positioning.** Cotton home textiles for rented city flats: bedsheets, cushion covers and
  blackout curtains that look good and survive frequent washing. Affordable to mid price.
- **Product lines.** Percale and sateen cotton bedsheet sets; cushion covers in seasonal prints;
  blackout and sheer curtains; a "move-in bundle" for new tenants.
- **Customers.** Renters in their twenties furnishing a first flat; families redecorating for
  festivals; hosts who let out rooms short-term.
- **Channels.** Marketplaces first, own website second, Pinterest and Instagram home-decor creators.
- **Competitors (fictional).** *SoftLoom* (premium hotel-style linen); *Duvetly* (low-price,
  very wide catalogue).
- **Voice.** Friendly, unfussy, upbeat about small spaces.
