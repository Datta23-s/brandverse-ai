with AI assistance and reviewed and edited by Kundan (`docs/evaluation-plan.md` §7, "Limitation of
the relevance set"). Eight are unanswerable by design and marked so; they are reported separately.
`queries.yaml` is derived from this file by `app.evaluation.relevance.parse_queries_md` and a test
fails if the two differ. Both are frozen in `queries.lock`.
